# Intelligent Networked Systems Lab

**INSLab** at **Ewha Womans University**

We design intelligent systems that learn, adapt, and collaborate across devices, networks, and modalities.

### Research Areas

Our research focuses on intelligent systems that learn, adapt, sense, and collaborate across devices, networks, and modalities.

- **Federated & Collaborative AI**
- **Multimodal AI**
- **Intelligent Sensing & Localization**
- **Trustworthy & Secure AI**

### Selected Projects

We develop learning systems for real-world networked environments, with a focus on efficient, adaptive, and collaborative intelligence.

More details about our research, projects, publications, and members will be available here soon.
