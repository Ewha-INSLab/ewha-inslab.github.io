## About

I am a professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Ewha Womans University. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University.

My research interests are in AI-driven edge computing architecture and network systems, including collaborative and federated learning, continual learning, multimodal AI, intelligent sensing and localization, networked intelligence, and edge computing systems.

I lead the <strong>Intelligent Networked Systems Lab (INSLab)</strong> at Ewha Womans University.

I am looking for passionate undergraduate, master's, and Ph.D. students, as well as postdoctoral researchers, who are interested in conducting research in these areas.

## Research Interests

* Federated and collaborative learning
* Continual learning and knowledge transfer
* Multimodal and edge AI
* Intelligent sensing and indoor localization
* Networked intelligence and edge computing
* AI-driven network and IoT systems
