Our research focuses on intelligent systems that learn, adapt, sense, and collaborate across devices, networks, and modalities.

## Research Areas

### Federated & Collaborative AI

Learning and knowledge transfer across distributed devices and heterogeneous environments.

### Multimodal AI

Learning and reasoning across multiple modalities, including vision, audio, language, and sensor data.

### Intelligent Sensing & Localization

Intelligent sensing, spatial understanding, and localization using multimodal and networked signals.

### Trustworthy & Secure AI

Robust, privacy-aware, and trustworthy learning for intelligent networked systems.
