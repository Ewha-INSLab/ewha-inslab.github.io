## Research Areas

### Federated & Collaborative AI

We develop learning and knowledge transfer methods for intelligent systems that collaborate across distributed and heterogeneous devices, with a focus on federated learning, continual learning, and device-to-device knowledge transfer.

### Multimodal AI

We develop AI systems that learn from and integrate diverse modalities, including vision, audio, language, and sensor data, with an emphasis on efficient multimodal intelligence at the edge.

### Intelligent Sensing & Localization

We explore intelligent sensing and localization using audio, wireless signals, and other environmental signals to enable robust spatial understanding and context-aware intelligence.

### Trustworthy & Secure AI

We investigate privacy, robustness, security, and trustworthy learning techniques for reliable AI systems operating in distributed and networked environments.
